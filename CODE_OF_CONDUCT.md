# Code of Conduct

We want Footnote's community to be welcoming to everyone who tracks work, whatever their job, experience, background, identity,
age, ability, or language. This text is written in our own words and is inspired by the Contributor Covenant.

## Expected
- Be kind, patient and respectful. Assume good intent; ask before assuming bad.
- Explain things in plain words. "I don't understand" is always a welcome question.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully; focus on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy. Don't share other people's information, logs or screenshots without permission, and redact tokens before pasting.

## Not acceptable
- Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, sexualised language or attention, doxxing, or sustained disruption.
- Sharing someone's private information; trolling; deliberately misleading others about security issues.

## Scope and enforcement
This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone represents the project.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate it and may warn, temporarily limit or ban people who do. To report a
problem, use the repository's private reporting option (Security tab) or contact a maintainer directly; reports are handled
privately and with care. Until a dedicated address is published, report conduct concerns privately through GitHub: open this repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability** and start the title with "Conduct:" (only the maintainers can read it).
